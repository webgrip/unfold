# Expressive product sites

Make the product recognizable, understandable and worth inspecting. A request for more impact is a composition problem before it is an animation problem.

## Ground the promise

Inspect the current site, repository, executable paths, product documentation and brand sources. Establish a small claim ledger: claim → evidence → implemented/planned/uncertain → permitted wording. Accepted architecture is not proof of completed migration. A deterministic fixture is not a production benchmark.

When access or evidence is missing, distinguish supplied facts from independently verified ones. Continue useful design work with explicit assumptions; leave exact artifacts and unsupported claims pending instead of inventing them.

Identify the audience's first decision and the proof they need to make it. Keep a plain category statement near the memorable hook. Preserve availability, limitations and source access where they affect that decision.

## Research mechanisms

Use current primary sources and inspect rendered examples when drawing visual conclusions. Combine relevant product sites with a few adjacent references from physical products, editorial design or events. Record the observed mechanism, why it fits this product, what to adapt and what not to borrow. Separate observation from inference; visual preference does not establish conversion impact.

Research until a defensible direction emerges. Save a compact rationale with source links, rather than an unbounded inspiration list. Read [the Unfold case study](references/unfold-case-study.md) only when an example of this reasoning would help; its palette, paper metaphor and section order are not a template.

## Choose a visual thesis

Find one consequential product artifact or action that can carry the visual identity. Connect the promise, the brand, the evidence and the interaction through that idea.

Judge the still composition first, with motion disabled, on desktop and mobile. Look for a dominant silhouette, deliberate scale differences, a controlled focal accent and changing density across chapters. If an already competent design feels flat, reconsider hierarchy, asymmetry and spatial composition before adding effects. Preserve brand invariants; distinguish illustrative geometry from an official mark.

## Make evidence the spectacle

Follow a representative object through cause and consequence: its input, action, output, checks and decision. Expose meaningful detail at the moment it answers a visitor's question. Use real artifacts with provenance; label simulated or recorded demonstrations beside them. Never fabricate live activity, customer proof, success, spend or benchmarks.

Recompose for touch and narrow screens instead of shrinking a desktop scene. A smaller, front-facing or selectable view may communicate more than a scaled diorama. Keep essential content readable without animation or scripting.

## Give motion a contract

For each effect, specify its trigger, explanatory purpose, end state, interruption, reduced-motion alternative and available controls. Prefer finite gestures and self-paced inspection when they fit the story. Match visible controls to effects that actually exist at that breakpoint and preference setting.

Use one authoritative selection state for evidence, illustration, controls and accessibility state. Preserve focused content across playback and responsive reflow. Reserve layout before enhancement; visual ambition must not introduce page jumps.

## Validate and retain the work

Read [implementation and validation](references/implementation-and-validation.md) when building or reviewing interactions. It contains regression traps observed in a shipped implementation and a practical test matrix.

Verify the final production build with appropriate repository gates, meaningful behavior tests, automated accessibility scans and rendered interaction checks. Report the actual environment, states exercised, measured results and gaps. Lab scores do not establish field performance or accessibility conformance.

Keep the implementation, concise research rationale and validation record together in the project. Follow the user's requested delivery scope and the repository's release process; creating a design does not itself authorize publishing it.
