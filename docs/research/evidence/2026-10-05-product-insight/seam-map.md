## 1. How the front end is served, and its CSP

**Serving**
- Unfold's single Node HTTP server serves the front end itself. G/apps/unfold/src/http.ts:554 holds an allow-list map: `/` → index.html, app.js, shell.js, now.js, ploeg.js, ploeg-activity.js, delivery.js, styles.css, the favicons, the webmanifest, og-image and fonts.
- Other files must match `browserModule` (http.ts:29): `core|views|styles/*.js|css`, `cards/...`, `vendor/three/*.js`. They are served by `StaticFiles.serve` (http.ts:557; G/apps/unfold/src/static.ts:37-50) with a sha256 ETag, `Cache-Control: no-cache` and gzip.
- There is no bundler or build step. G/apps/unfold/public/index.html:28 loads `<script src="core/theme.js">` and :30 loads `<script type="module" src="app.js">`.

**CSP on every response** (G/apps/unfold/src/http.ts:139):
`default-src 'self'; script-src 'self'; style-src 'self'; img-src 'self' data:; connect-src 'self'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'none'; frame-ancestors 'none'; form-action 'self'`
- http.ts:136-138 also sets nosniff, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` and `X-Frame-Options: DENY`.
- **worker-src is not set.** It falls back to script-src, so effectively 'self'. `new Worker`, `SharedWorker` and `serviceWorker` have zero hits in public/ and src/.
- There is no nonce or hash. The only "nonce" in src is the OIDC nonce (src/oidc.ts:85-141).
- Uploaded card assets get their own CSP, `default-src 'none'; sandbox` (http.ts:70; documented in G/apps/unfold/docs/contracts/card-themes.md:65).
- Documented policy: G/apps/unfold/docs/browser-ui.md:320-329. It says "No CDN, remote font or remote image" (:326), and the browser check fails on any CSP violation or console error (:329).

**Third-party scripts are not allowed anywhere in Unfold.** ADRs keep refusing them:
- G/apps/unfold/docs/adrs/0026-...md:18 ("The CSP stays as it is")
- 0028-...md:18 ("no CDN, no inline style or script, no import map") and :80 (CDN rejected)
- 0031-...md:18
- browser-ui.md:263 ("no third-party icon set")
- The only external URLs in public/ JS are example.org, litellm.example and *.invalid placeholders.

**Marketing site (apps/site)**
- G/apps/site/astro.config.mjs:43-61 configures Astro `security.csp` with directives `default-src 'self'; img-src 'self' data:; font-src 'self'; connect-src 'self'; base-uri 'self'; form-action 'self'; object-src 'none'`, and script and style resources limited to 'self'.
- Astro's CSP feature emits hashes for its own scripts and styles. That is an inference: I did not inspect the build output.
- G/apps/site/public/_headers:1-7 adds `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none'`, `Permissions-Policy: geolocation=(), camera=(), microphone=(), browsing-topics=()`, COOP same-origin and `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. It has no worker-src.
- The site demo has a meta CSP: G/apps/site/public/demo/index.html:5 (`default-src 'self'; script-src 'self'; style-src 'self'; img-src 'self' data:; connect-src 'self'; font-src 'self'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'none'; form-action 'self'`), generated by G/apps/site/scripts/build-demo.mjs:16,89.
