# What an Ask costs and how well it answers

Date: 11 October 2026, on homelab with Ploeg 0.2.0-rc.14 and Unfold 0.4.0-rc.60, after `fireworks-glm-5p3-flash` got its price in LiteLLM (homelab-cluster #728). This is a record for spike VIK-1963. The decision it informs is the proposed Ask Allowance and per-Ask Budget in [ADR-0006](../adr/adr-0006-the-ticket-is-the-billing-unit.md#decided-numbers), from [ADR-0031](../adr/adr-0031-people-ask-about-a-work-item-through-a-metered-read-only-ask-paid-from-a-monthly-allowance.md).

> **Method.** Unfold's own `AskService` ran inside the Unfold pod with an in-memory store. Each Ask was admitted by Ploeg as actor `spike-1963`, answered by GLM 5.3 Flash with the Work Item Brief, and finished. That is 14 Asks on 7 Work Items across the bronze, silver, unfold and copper Teams, in the states done and withdrawn, with two questions each. Token counts come from the gateway's `usage`. Cost is computed at the Fireworks list price (US$ 0,15 in, 0,50 out per million tokens). The raw answers are in [evidence/2026-10-11-ask-cost/asks.jsonl](evidence/2026-10-11-ask-cost/asks.jsonl). One Work Item (#1) could not be read: Unfold refused its detail because of a pre-Shift Run, fixed alongside this record. No Work Item was in `needs_human` or `awaiting_review` at the time, so those questions are untested.

## Verdict

An Ask costs about **US$ 0,0002**: about 570 input tokens and 170 output tokens on average, and at most US$ 0,0003. The estimate in ADR-0031 (US$ 0,001, from a 6.000-token brief) was 6× too high, because a real brief is about 500 tokens.

| Measure | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Asks | 14 on 7 Work Items |
| Input tokens | average 573, maximum 989 |
| Output tokens | average 173, maximum 325 |
| Cost per Ask | average US$ 0,00017, maximum US$ 0,00027 |
| Total | US$ 0,0024 |

At this cost the proposed numbers have wide headroom. The per-Ask Budget of US$ 0,02 is about 75× the most expensive Ask, and the monthly allowance of US$ 2 covers about 11.000 Asks. The allowance therefore works as an abuse limit, not as a meter people will notice.

## Answer quality

All 14 answers stayed within the record, and said "the record does not say" when it did not. Two faults came from the brief, not the model, and are fixed in the same change as this record:

- On done or withdrawn work the brief described the pull request as "open" (#2, #28), because Ploeg reports that a pull request exists but not whether it was merged or closed. The brief now says it is part of the finished work, or was left when the work was withdrawn.
- A Shift close reason without a phrase showed up as a code, such as "the plan was exhausted". Every close reason Ploeg sends now has a phrase.

## Options for the owner

- Keep US$ 2 a month and US$ 0,02 per Ask. They are safe and simple, and nobody will hit them by asking normally.
- Lower the per-Ask Budget to US$ 0,005. That is still 18× the most expensive Ask, and it reduces what one runaway or abusive Ask can cost.
- Measure again once Work Items are waiting on a person, because those briefs carry more Runs and longer summaries.
