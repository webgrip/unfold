# Unfold: name and mark

"Unfold" and the Unfold mark, the Vouwvlieger, identify this project. This page sets out how they may be used.

**Status: proposed.** [ADR-0014](../adr/adr-0014-the-unfold-name-and-mark-are-trademarks-not-cc-licensed-artwork.md) records the decision, and it stays proposed until a trademark search is done: Squarespace lists "Unfold" among its own trademarks. Until then the project claims no registered right in the name, and this page describes how the maintainers intend to act.

The software is [Apache-2.0](../../LICENSE), and so are the files in this directory. Section 6 of that licence grants no rights in trade names or marks, and that carve-out is deliberate. This page fills it.

Ploeg and Vloer, Unfold's parts, keep their own policies: [Ploeg](../../apps/ploeg/docs/brand/TRADEMARK.md) and [Vloer](../../apps/vloer/docs/brand/TRADEMARK.md).

## You may, without asking

- Reproduce the **unmodified** mark, wordmark or lockup to refer to Unfold: in articles, talks, slides, README badges, ecosystem and integration listings, comparison tables and screenshots.
- State truthfully that your software **works with**, **supports**, **integrates with** or **is built on** Unfold.
- Link to the project using the name and mark.
- Scale the assets, and use any of the supplied colour, night, monochrome or tile variants.
- Redistribute an **unmodified** Unfold as Unfold: a distribution package, a container image or a chart mirror.

No permission request, no notification, no fee.

## You may not, without written permission

- Ship a **modified or forked** distribution under the name Unfold or under the mark. Fork the code freely, as Apache-2.0 allows, but give it your own name.
- Use the name or mark in a way that implies **endorsement, affiliation, sponsorship or certification** by the Unfold project.
- Use the name or mark, or anything confusingly similar, as **your own** product name, company name, domain name, app-store listing or social handle.
- **Alter** the mark: change its proportions, the crease, the corner radii or the two-colour split; recolour it outside the supplied palette; rotate it; add effects; or combine it with another mark into a new one.
- Present the mark as the most prominent element on material that is not the project's own, in a way that suggests the material comes from us.

## Using it correctly

[README.md](README.md) in this directory carries the construction, the palette with measured contrast, the lockups, clear space and minimum sizes. The short version:

- Clear space: a quarter of the mark's height on every side.
- Minimum size: 16 px for the mark (use `favicon.svg` below 32 px), 96 px wide for the horizontal lockup, 64 px wide for the stacked one.
- Never re-set the wordmark in a live font. Use `wordmark.svg`; it is outlined.

## The typeface is not ours

The wordmark is drawn in **Archivo** by Héctor Gatti and Omnibus-Type, licensed [SIL OFL 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/). That licence governs the typeface and is unaffected by this page. Redistributing the font follows the OFL, not this policy.

## Questions and permission

Anything this page does not clearly allow, ask. Open an issue on the project forge or write to the maintainers. We would rather say yes to a specific request than have this page guessed at. If you spot a misuse, telling us is welcome and is not an accusation.
